## Prompt

Vintage gouache-and-ink game-card back design, perfectly symmetrical so it
reads as the reverse of a playing card: ornate art-deco sunburst behind a
central emblem of a fedora above a crossed tommy gun and violin case, framed
by stepped ziggurat corner motifs, drawn in muted sepia and oxblood linework on
aged textured paper with faint cigar-smoke swirls woven into the rays, subtle
film grain, thin double art-deco border frame around the card, painterly
visible brushwork, no characters, no photorealism, no text, square 1:1 game card.
