## Prompt

Vintage gouache-and-ink game-card back design, perfectly symmetrical and
centerless so it reads as the reverse of a playing card: playful cartoon noir
emblem of a tiny bowler hat above a banana-curved tommy gun crossed with a
cream pie, radiating bouncy art-deco sunburst rays with little stars, drawn in
muted sepia linework with rubber-hose wobble on aged textured paper, subtle
film grain, thin double art-deco border frame around the card, painterly
visible brushwork, no characters, no photorealism, no text, square 1:1 game card.
